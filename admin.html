Products, pricing model, and payment links. Changes go live within about a minute.
The password is the ADMIN_PASSWORD value set in Netlify environment variables. It is checked server-side on every request.

Pricing model

Dual pricing shows BOTH prices on every listing before checkout — the bank-pay price and the card price. Colorado caps card surcharges (2% or actual cost of acceptance); confirm your differential and presentation against current statute and card-brand rules before enabling above 2%.

Add product

Create the Stripe Payment Link on the product in your Stripe dashboard (enable "US bank account" as a payment method there for the ACH link, 0.8% fee capped at $5). Leave any link blank to hide that button on the site.
