Our Story

Fran's Boutique is a family-run shop built on one habit: never walking past something wonderful.

What condition are pieces in The Find?

Every vintage piece is inspected, cleaned, and described honestly — condition notes and true-to-life photos on every listing.

Who makes the jewelry and art in The Studio?

Independent artisans working in small batches. Each listing names the maker and the materials.

How fast do you ship?

Orders pack within two business days and ship from Colorado with tracking on every package.

Can I return something?

Yes — unworn, undamaged items within 14 days. One-of-a-kind pieces go back behind the glass for someone else to find.
