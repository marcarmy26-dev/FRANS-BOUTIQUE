About the Artist

Where Nature Finds Its Canvas

Tesa Molyneux, an Alabama native, has been drawing and painting as far back as she remembers. She is intrigued by the play of light and shadow in nature. Much of her work evokes a sense of peacefulness and serenity, and the feeling that you want to be there.

Tesa enjoys experimenting with different techniques. Her most recent works are so thickly textured that you can't help but touch them. Go ahead. She doesn't mind.

Tesa now resides in colorful Colorado where she is inspired by the beauty of God's creation. She hopes her works bring you joy and peace as you view them.
